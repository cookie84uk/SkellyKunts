A roll is 100,000 $SKELLY. 10% is burned, the rest goes into the pot. There is no house: everything in the pot goes back out as prizes. The result is fixed by a public randomness beacon 30 seconds after you roll and verified on-chain — nobody knows it when you pay, and nobody can hide it.

Odds are on-chain. A prize the pot can't cover drops out and the chances re-spread over the rest — if that happens between your roll and its settlement, you are re-drawn across what's left.
