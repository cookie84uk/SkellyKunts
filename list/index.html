Questions people ask

Is it really free?

For the GTD round, yes. When the list closes we go through every wallet on it and each one lands in a round: GTD (a spot held for you) or FCFS (first come, first served). Real accounts with real activity go GTD first. Anyone not on the list can mint in the public round at 0.0015 ETH. Gas on Robinhood Chain is a fraction of a cent, so you need a tiny bit of ETH on Robinhood Chain in that wallet when you mint.

Which wallet?

Any normal Ethereum wallet: MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet, Phantom's EVM side, a hardware wallet. Paste the address you'll mint with. It has to be the same wallet on mint day; the list is by address.

How do I get ETH on Robinhood Chain?

Bridge a little ETH over from Ethereum or another chain. A dollar's worth covers gas for a very long time.

When is the mint?

Soon. The date lands here and on the board as a countdown the moment it's set. Follow @SkellyKuntz.

Do I have to post?

Yes. The post is how we know the wallet belongs to a real person with a real X account. No permissions are asked: you post yourself, we only read the public post.

It says X can't show you my post

X hides some accounts' posts from the tools we read with. Your link is saved and a person checks it by hand, usually within the day. Nothing else to do. If you'd rather not wait, turn off "mark media you post as sensitive" in X settings and post once more.

Can I use two wallets?

One wallet per X account, one X account per wallet. Posting again with the same wallet just updates it.

I held a Skelly on Ink

Complete the form we posted on X. Everyone who fills it gets their Skelly airdropped before the mint, to the wallet they gave, checked against the Ink holder snapshot. No form, no airdrop. You can still get on the list with the same wallet for a shot at another one.
